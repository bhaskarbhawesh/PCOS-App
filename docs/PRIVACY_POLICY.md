# Privacy Policy for Compite

_Last updated: [date]_

Compite ("the app") is developed by [Your name / company]. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. **Information we collect.** We do not collect, store or transmit your personal information. The habits, check-ins, cycle entries, notes and settings you enter in the app are stored only on your device.
2. **How your information is used.** Your information is used only inside the app to show your routine, reminders, charts and reports.
3. **Sharing.** We do not sell, rent or share your information. If you choose to export a PDF or CSV, you decide where it goes through your device's share options.
4. **Third parties.** The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services.
5. **Notifications.** The app sends local reminders that you set. They are created on your device and do not use any server.
6. **Face ID.** If you turn on the app lock, Face ID or your passcode is checked by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.
7. **Data deletion.** You can delete all app data in Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.
8. **Backups.** Your device or iCloud backup settings may back up app data according to your Apple settings. We do not have access to those backups.
9. **Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
10. **Medical disclaimer.** The app does not provide medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional about your health.
11. **Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
