## Description

Build a PCOS routine you can actually keep.

Compite is a calm habit and reminder app for people with PCOS. Pick the habits you want to build, get gentle reminders you control, log how you feel in about 10 seconds, and bring a clear summary to your next appointment.

PAY ONCE. NO SUBSCRIPTION. NO ADS. NO ACCOUNT.
Your data stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build your own daily routine: movement, sleep, water, meals, medication or supplement reminders (you type them in)
• Gentle reminders with quiet hours and a daily limit, so you're never spammed
• Quick daily check-in: mood, energy, pain, skin and sleep
• Cycle log made for irregular cycles, with no limit on how long a period lasts
• Weekly and monthly charts that show your habits and how you've been feeling
• A one-tap PDF summary to share with your doctor
• Dark mode, large text and VoiceOver support

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your entries are stored only on your device. You can export or delete everything at any time.

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone living with PCOS (sometimes also called PMOS), or tracking irregular cycles and symptoms, who wants a simple routine without monthly fees.

IMPORTANT
Compite is for tracking and reminders only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow your doctor's guidance.

Questions or ideas? Email [support email]. I read every message.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Data Not Collected. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

## Notes for App Review

- No account or login is required. All data is stored locally on the device.
- The app uses local notifications chosen by the user for habit reminders.
- The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and does not suggest medications or doses. Medication/supplement reminders contain text entered by the user.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- Face ID (if enabled by the user) is used only to lock the app.
- Settings › Privacy & data lets users export or delete all data.
